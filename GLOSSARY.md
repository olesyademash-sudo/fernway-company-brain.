# Glossary

Standard Fernway terms, abbreviations and internal jargon. Keep entries in
alphabetical order, and link to the doc that explains each term in depth.

| Term | Meaning | More |
|---|---|---|
| **Account** | A customer, usually a café or restaurant group. One account has many **venues**. Contracts are per account. | [Product overview](docs/product/overview.md#accounts--venues) |
| **Activation gap** | The internal mismatch where Sales, CS and Metabase each define "activation" differently, so their numbers don't match. | [Product overview](docs/product/overview.md#activation-gap) |
| **Friday freeze** | The strict rule: no deploys on Fridays. | [Mission & values](docs/company/mission-values.md#5-no-deploys-on-friday) |
| **Ghost shift** | A published shift that nobody claimed. Our top support complaint. | [Product overview](docs/product/overview.md#ghost-shift) |
| **Importer, the** | The CSV staff-import tool used during onboarding. | [CSV importer runbook](runbooks/csv-importer-fix.md) |
| **Mara-check** | CEO (Mara) approval, required for any pricing exception or discount beyond the standard price and the annual-prepay discount. | [Sales & pricing](docs/go-to-market/sales-pricing.md#discount-rules) |
| **Pay rules** | Per-country break and overtime configurations, set during onboarding. | [Customer onboarding](docs/operations/processes.md#customer-onboarding) |
| **T2L (Time to Live)** | The time from contract signed to first schedule published. Our core onboarding metric. Target: under a day (in practice, sometimes 1–3 days). | [Product overview](docs/product/overview.md#t2l-time-to-live) |
| **Venue** | A single location within an **account**. Billing is per venue. | [Product overview](docs/product/overview.md#accounts--venues) |

# Product Overview

What Fernway Shifts is, who it's for, what it does, and the product terms we
use every day.

## Fernway Shifts

**Fernway Shifts** is shift planning and labor-cost forecasting software for
hospitality groups.

**Target audience:** independent café and restaurant groups with
**3–20 locations**.

## Core capabilities

| Capability | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Shift schedule builder** | Lets managers build shift schedules for their venues. |
| **Pay rules** | Automatically checks schedules against local **break and overtime rules**, per country. |
| **Wage bill projection** | Projects the wage bill for a schedule **before it is published**. |

## Accounts & venues

```
Account (customer group)
├── Venue A
├── Venue B
└── Venue C …
```

- **Account:** the customer, usually a café or restaurant group. One account
  has many venues.
- **Venue:** a single location within an account.

| | Level |
|---|---|
| **Contracts** | Per **account** |
| **Billing** | Per **venue**: **€39 / month / venue** |

## Terminology

### T2L (Time to Live)

How long it takes to go from **contract signed** to **first schedule
published**. T2L is our core metric. See
[Mission & Values](../company/mission-values.md).

### Ghost shift

A **published shift that nobody claimed**. Ghost shifts are our **top
customer complaint**.

### Activation gap

"Activation" means different things to different teams. That mismatch is the
activation gap:

| Who | Definition of "activated" |
|---|---|
| **Sales** | First schedule published |
| **Customer Success** (Lukas) | A full week scheduled |
| **Metabase** | Tracks yet another metric. *Which one isn't documented yet.* |

> ⚠️ **Open question:** we have no single agreed definition of activation, so
> numbers from Sales, CS and Metabase can't be compared directly. Agree on one
> definition and document it here.

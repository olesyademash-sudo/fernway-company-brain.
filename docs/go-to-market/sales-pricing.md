# Sales & Pricing

Where we sell, how we price, who approves discounts, and how a lead becomes
a customer.

## Pricing

| Plan | Price |
|---|---|
| **Monthly** | **€39 / venue / month** |
| **Annual prepay** | **15% discount** on the monthly price |

Contracts are per account; billing is per venue. See
[Operations: Billing & payments](../operations/processes.md#billing--payments).

## Discount rules

- The **standard price** (€39) and the **annual-prepay discount** (15%) need
  no approval.
- **Any price below that** needs **Mara's approval**. Internally this is the
  **"Mara-check"**.

> No Mara-check, no discount. Don't promise a customer a lower price before
> Mara has approved it.

## Sales pipeline

```
Website form / Intercom chat
  → HubSpot
  → Priya qualifies the lead
  → Daniel runs the demo
  → Mara joins (deals over 10 venues)
```

| # | Stage | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Inbound lead** | — | Comes from the website form or an Intercom chat. |
| 2 | **Logged in HubSpot** | — | All leads and deals live in HubSpot. |
| 3 | **Qualification** | **Priya** | See the SLA note below. |
| 4 | **Demo** | **Daniel** | |
| 5 | **Large deals** | **Mara** | Joins for deals **over 10 venues**. |

Once a contract is signed in HubSpot,
[customer onboarding](../operations/processes.md#customer-onboarding) starts.
